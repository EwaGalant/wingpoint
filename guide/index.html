Getting around

Book your Uber ahead. There are only a handful of drivers on the island, especially early mornings, evenings and when a ferry lands. For a ferry, airport run or dinner reservation, schedule the ride in the Uber app the day before (tap Later next to "Where to?"), not five minutes before you need it.

Uber & Lyft

reserve in advance

Both apps work on the island, but cars are few and often come over on the ferry from Seattle, so an on-demand request can take 20+ minutes or go unanswered.

  • Schedule it: open Uber, tap Later, pick a time, then set pickup at the house.
  • For Sea-Tac, the ferry plus a Seattle-side Uber or the Link light rail is often quicker than a car around the Sound.
  • Coming back from Winslow late? Book the return when you set out.

BI Ride + the Pingo app

island shuttle · $2

Kitsap Transit's on-demand minibus covers most of the island: the ferry dock, Winslow, Bloedel Reserve, Lynwood Center, Battle Point and more. Adults $2, 18 and under ride free.

  1. Download Ride Pingo (App Store or Google Play) and create an account.
  2. Enter the house as pickup and your destination; choose now or schedule up to 7 days ahead.
  3. Walk to the pickup point the app gives you (the bus can't use some driveways or dead ends) and track it live.
  4. Catching a ferry? Choose Transit Connect and enter the sailing time.

Hours: weekdays 8:45am to 3:30pm, Saturdays 9am to 7pm (book via the app after 4pm). Or call 1-844-424-7433, 8am to 4pm. Schedule & Sunday service

The ferry to Seattle

35 minutes · walk-on

Washington State Ferries run from Bainbridge to Seattle's Colman Dock throughout the day, roughly every hour. The terminal is roughly 18 minutes on foot from the house.

  • Walk-on fares are collected only on the Seattle side, so the ride into Seattle is free and you pay on the way back.
  • Check sailing times in the WSDOT app or at wsdot.com/ferries; arrive 10 minutes early on foot.
  • From Colman Dock, Pike Place Market is a 10-minute walk.

Groceries & delivery

Instacart · DoorDash
  • Instacart delivers on the island, including from Safeway on High School Rd, usually same day.
  • Town & Country Market (Winslow) is the best local grocery for produce, the deli and wine, an easy stop on a walk into town.
  • DoorDash has the widest restaurant choice here: Nirvana, Bainbridge Thai, Yoyo Poke, Westside Pizza and others. Uber Eats has fewer island restaurants.
  • Many places on this page also do takeout; call ahead and pick up on your way back.
