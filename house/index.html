The house, floor by floor

Each floor could be a home on its own. Together they give a family, a team on assignment or friends who work remotely room to spread out, with original oil paintings and sculpture on almost every wall.

Main floor

For cooking, dining and long evenings

  • Open kitchen with an island gas cooktop, Sub-Zero refrigerator and bar seating
  • Two living rooms: one around a stone gas fireplace, one opening to the deck through French doors
  • Dining room that seats eight, under large original canvases
  • Laundry room with washer and dryer, powder room and entry hall
  • Deck with a dining table, BBQ grill, lounge seating, hanging chair, two large umbrellas and a patio heater

Upper floor

For rest and focused work

  • Primary suite under a vaulted ceiling, with a king bed and walk-in closet
  • Spa bathroom with a jetted tub, a shower with built-in steam sauna, and skylights
  • Second bedroom with a double bed
  • Two private offices, one with garden and golf-course views
  • Two more full bathrooms, a second walk-in closet and a laundry closet

Hot tub, gym and garden

The everyday luxuries of a long stay: somewhere to train in the morning, soak at night and walk out under the trees.

Hot tub

A private soak, all year

An outdoor hot tub on the garden patio, open 24 hours and in use year-round, with room for up to seven. Winter evenings in the steam, under the cedars, are the point of a stay here.

Gym

Train without leaving home

A private home gym with exercise equipment and yoga mats, plus its own half bath. Take the mats out to the garden on a dry morning.

Garden

Forest on three sides

A lawn and patio wrapped in tall firs and cedars, with lounge seating and a hanging chair. Deer, rabbits and bald eagles pass through, and the golf course paths next door make an easy evening walk.

Space

6,000 square feet

Three full floors: two living rooms, a dining room, five bedrooms, two offices, five baths and a deck, so everyone has a room to retreat to.

House rules

The home holds a private art collection and library. Enjoy them, and please leave the artwork where it hangs.

No pets, no smoking or vaping, no parties or events.

Up to 10 guests. Only registered guests stay overnight.

The garden is unfenced and wild. Deer, rabbits and bald eagles visit, so keep an eye on small children outdoors.

Golf course paths are yours for walks early in the morning or in the evening when no one is playing.
