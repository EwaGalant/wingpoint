🗝️Arrival

11391 NE Wing Point Way, Bainbridge Island, WA 98110 · Open in Google Maps

Check-in is from 4:00 pm. Entry is keyless: your door code arrives as an Airbnb message before you arrive.

Getting in

  1. This is a freestanding house. Your apartment is the lowest floor, with a private entrance at the back. Don't use the main front door.
  2. Walk around the house using the grassy incline with stone steps on the right (recommended) or the stairs on the left. Signs point the way.
  3. On the grassy route, the first doors you reach are the patio doors. They're locked from the inside, so keep walking.
  4. You'll also pass the gym door. Keep going to the door with the smart lock and enter your code.

The stairs and the grassy path can be slippery or muddy when it rains. Please take care.

♨️Hot tub

Your private hot tub is on the garden patio, open all year and 24 hours, with room for up to seven.

Using the cover

  • Unhook all the hooks around the tub before lifting the lid.
  • Lay the lid and cover upside down on the ground, clean side up, so no dirt gets into the water.
  • Put both the lid and the cover back after every use. It keeps the water warm and saves energy.

Control panel

  • Power: if the tub is off, press the power button until the lights come on.
  • Unlock: press and hold the lock button for 3–5 seconds before using the panel.
  • Temperature: press the temperature button, then use the arrows. You can switch between °F and °C. Heating can take several hours, so keep the lid on while it warms up.
  • Bubbles: press the bubbles button to start the jets.

Please take a quick shower before getting in, and rinse off afterwards. The water contains chlorine, which can dry your skin, and there's lotion in the bathroom.

🏋️Gym

The gym is next to the apartment and you're welcome to use it. Its key hangs by your apartment entrance; please lock up and return it to the hook after each visit. The gym has a small toilet (no shower). Yoga mats are there for the gym or the garden.

The gym also holds some of the owners' storage, including spare paintings. Please don't move or touch personal items. The owners may occasionally use it.

🌲Garden & golf course

The patio, garden and hanging chair are yours. The hanging chair holds up to 220 lbs; please climb in from the sides, not the end.

You can walk the golf course paths from the back of the house, but only early in the morning (before about 7–8 am) or in the early evening (around 7–8 pm, later in summer), and only when no golfers are on the course. Once golfers are out, please stay off: the club doesn't allow it and stray balls are a real risk. Playing golf isn't permitted.

The backyard isn't fenced. You may see bald eagles, deer, rabbits, squirrels, tree frogs and sometimes sea otters, and occasionally coyotes at night. Please don't leave small children outside alone.

🚮Trash & safety

Trash and recycling

Bins are on the left side of the house. Please bag all trash. Collection is on Tuesdays: the black bin (trash) every week, the blue bin (recycling) every other week.

Safety

  • Smoke detector, carbon monoxide detector and a noise monitor are installed.
